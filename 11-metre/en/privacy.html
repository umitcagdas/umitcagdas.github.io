Privacy Policy · 11 Metre

11 Metre (Spot Kick Rivals) is a penalty shootout game by Ümit Çağdaş. This policy covers the game and its website.

Profile and game data

Playing as a guest creates a game profile with a random ID. We keep your chosen nickname, match results, rank, training progress, energy, and locker items to run the game, find matches, and save progress. Your player name, profile appearance, and results can be shown to opponents or in standings.

Sign in with Apple or Google

Linking an account is optional. To let you play across devices, we receive your account identifier from the provider you choose. We do not request your email address, Apple or Google profile name, or password during sign-in. If Apple issues a refresh token on verified sign-in, we store it encrypted so we can revoke your permission.

Purchases and measurement

Apple or Google processes payments. We use product and transaction information with the relevant store to deliver, restore, or verify a refund for a pack. We do not receive card details. Our game server measures matchmaking waits and duel counts as daily totals. Measurement records do not contain player or match IDs. Game measurements are on for new installations; switch them off in Account to exclude future matches.

Usage analytics on iOS

Google Firebase Analytics is on by default in iOS versions that include this feature. It processes app opens, sessions, the names of screens viewed, matchmaking, duels and training activity, the app version and approximate region. Analytics uses a random identifier for this installation. We do not send your player name, game account ID or payment records to analytics. We use this data to improve the game. Switching analytics off in Account stops new measurement and clears analytics data on this device.

Ads

Google AdMob serves the rewarded ads you choose to watch and ads between matches. Google may process your IP address, advertising identifiers, ad interactions and technical data for ad delivery, frequency limits and measurement. Personalized ads depend on Apple tracking permission on iOS, your in-game personalization choice on Android and any applicable regional consent. You can play and earn energy rewards if you decline. We send Google a random ad session code to verify energy rewards. You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings. Android personalization and available Google ad privacy options are in Account.

Security and retention

Game profiles and progress are held on our own servers. The mobile app uses HTTPS. We store hashes of session tokens and guest deletion codes. Profile data needed for gameplay stays until you delete the account. Sessions last up to 90 days; sign-in attempts expire within minutes. Access to necessary security and error logs is restricted. Event and installation level analytics data is retained for 2 months; aggregate statistics may remain longer.

Unlinking and deletion

Unlink a provider or delete your game account and its data from our active systems in Account. You can also delete your account on the web. An opponent’s result may remain without your name or identity. The stores’ own payment records and counts without account identifiers are separate from your game account. We may keep limited records as needed for security, fraud prevention, or legal recordkeeping.

How Google uses data from partner apps

When you contact support, we use your contact details and message to review and respond to your request.
