Privacy Policy · 11 Metre

11 Metre (Spot Kick Rivals) is a penalty shootout game by Ümit Çağdaş. This policy covers the game and its website.

Profile and game data

Playing as a guest creates a game profile with a random ID. We keep your chosen nickname, match results, rank, training progress, energy, and locker items to run the game, find matches, and save progress. Your player name, profile appearance, and results can be shown to opponents or in standings.

Sign in with Apple or Google

Linking an account is optional. To let you play across devices, we receive your account identifier from the provider you choose. We do not request your email address, Apple or Google profile name, or password during sign-in. If Apple issues a refresh token on verified sign-in, we store it encrypted so we can revoke your permission.

Purchases and measurement

Apple or Google processes payments. We use product and transaction information with the relevant store to deliver, restore, or verify a refund for a pack. We do not receive card details. You can opt in from Account to share matchmaking wait times and duel activity to help improve the game. This is off by default, and turning it off stops future measurement. We do not use this data to track you across other apps and websites.

Security and retention

Data is held on our own game servers. The mobile app uses HTTPS. We store hashes of session tokens and guest deletion codes. Profile data needed for gameplay stays until you delete the account. Sessions last up to 90 days; sign-in attempts expire within minutes. Access to necessary security and error logs is restricted. We limit data shared with Apple and Google to what is needed for sign-in and purchases, and expect equivalent data protection from them.

Unlinking and deletion

Unlink a provider or delete your game account and its data from our active systems in Account. You can also delete your account on the web. An opponent’s result may remain without your name or identity. The stores’ own payment records and counts without account identifiers are separate from your game account. We may keep limited records as needed for security, fraud prevention, or legal recordkeeping.

When you contact support, we use your contact details and message to review and respond to your request.
