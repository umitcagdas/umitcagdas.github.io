Setup

The desktop app must be open on your computer and the mobile app must be open on your phone.

1

Install the desktop app

Install Remotiq Desktop Agent on your Windows PC or Mac and leave it running.

2

Open the mobile app

Open Remotiq on your phone. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network.

3

Pair with the QR code

Scan the QR code shown on the desktop. Future connections can happen automatically.

Download

Install Remotiq Desktop first, then scan its QR code with Remotiq on your phone.

Privacy

Remote-control commands do not pass through a Remotiq internet relay. Optional Firebase usage and diagnostics can be disabled in Settings. The camera is used only for QR pairing; local-network access finds and connects to your computer.
