Market Sim Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Who operates the game

Market Sim is provided by Ümit Çağdaş. For privacy questions or requests, contact umitcagdas@gmail.com. This policy explains local game information, advertising services, in-app purchases and messages you choose to send to support.

Your game and preferences

You do not create a Market Sim account or sign in to the game with Apple or Google. Your career is saved on your device: progress, in-game cash and energy, stock, orders, shop layout, employees, goals and settings. Language is saved separately from the career. The current game does not upload your career to a publisher-operated game account or cloud-save service. Advertising and store services still make their own network connections.

Advertising data

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Depending on the platform, settings and permissions, advertising services may process IP addresses and approximate location, device or advertising identifiers, ad views and interactions, app interactions, crash reports and performance information. These support ad delivery, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention. Google explains its handling in the linked SDK disclosures and privacy notices.

Advertising choices

This release requests non-personalized ads and disables publisher first-party IDs. It does not request Apple tracking permission. Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks consent status at launch and presents a regional privacy message when required. Where required, reopen Ad choices from the pause screen to review your choices. Ads are requested only when UMP permits them. Non-personalized ads can still process data for delivery, measurement, diagnostics and fraud prevention.

Purchases and payment information

Apple App Store or Google Play processes payments through the store account used for the purchase. The game receives product, transaction and purchase-status information to grant items, prevent duplicate rewards and manage the Remove Ads entitlement. It stores the relevant transaction identifiers or token-derived references with the local save. The game does not ask for or receive full payment-card or bank-account details. Apple and Google handle their own payment records under their privacy notices; relevant order information may also be available through their developer and support processes.

Remove Ads and rewarded ads

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that disables automatic in-game ads. Rewarded ads remain optional and can still use advertising services when you choose them. This purchase does not change your consent choices or delete information already held by Google, Apple or another service provider. It is not a promise that all SDK initialization or network activity stops.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to understand and respond to your request. Send only information needed for the issue, such as device model, app version and a description. Do not send passwords, verification codes, complete payment details or purchase tokens. Support correspondence is also handled by the email service used to send and receive it.

Resetting or removing local data

Starting a new market asks for confirmation and replaces your career progress. It keeps the unspent portion of purchased cash and energy in addition to the new career’s starter funds, the Remove Ads entitlement, processed transaction references and your language choice. Earned currency is spent before purchased currency; already spent purchases are not granted again. This is a career reset, not deletion of every app record. Removing the app’s local data removes the local career and settings from that installation; device backups may affect what can later be restored. There is no publisher cloud-save service to recover a deleted career. Local deletion does not erase store purchase history, close your Apple or Google account, refund purchases or delete provider-held advertising information. Previously consumed cash and energy packs are not granted again by Restore.

Storage and retention

Local records remain on the device until replaced, reset or removed as described above. Advertising and store providers apply their own retention practices to the information they hold; see their notices. You can ask us about access to or deletion of support correspondence. A request sent to us does not itself delete records held independently by Apple or Google.

Questions and requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restrictions concerning personal information. Send requests to umitcagdas@gmail.com and identify Market Sim. We may need enough information to understand the request, without asking for your password. If you are a parent or guardian with a concern about a child’s information, use the same address. Changes to the game or its services may require this policy to be updated.
