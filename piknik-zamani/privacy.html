Privacy policy

Picnic Time is developed by Ümit Çağdaş. For privacy questions or support, contact umitcagdas@gmail.com.

Your progress, stars, settings and chosen player name are stored on your device. You can play without creating an account. You can remove local game data through your device’s app data controls or by uninstalling the app and its data.

The game checks connection status and uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform to load ads. Google may process IP addresses, coarse location inferred from IP addresses, device identifiers, app and ad interactions, crash logs, performance data and other diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, privacy preferences, security and diagnostics. The app applies child-directed treatment and requests non-personalized ads. When Google makes ad privacy options available, you can open them from the game settings. How Google uses information from partner apps

If you email us for support, we use your address and the details you provide to respond and resolve your request.

When Google’s form is available, you can change your advertising choices under Settings → Ad privacy options. For privacy or data deletion requests, use the email address above.
