Privacy policy

Picnic Time is developed by Ümit Çağdaş. For privacy questions or support, contact umitcagdas@gmail.com.

Your progress, stars, settings and chosen player name are stored on your device. You can play without creating an account. You can remove local game data through your device’s app data controls or by uninstalling the app and its data.

The game checks connection status and uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform to load ads. Google may process IP addresses, coarse location inferred from IP addresses, device identifiers, app and ad interactions, crash logs, performance data and other diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, privacy preferences, security and diagnostics. How Google uses information from partner apps

On iOS, eligible sessions may display Apple's tracking permission request. If you allow it, the advertising identifier may be used with your activity across other companies’ apps and websites to personalize ads and measure their performance. Denied or system-restricted permission uses non-personalized ad requests. Configured child audiences and sessions reported as restricted by the system receive child-directed ad protections. System tracking permission does not verify the player's age. The Android sample configuration retains child-directed, non-personalized ads.

You can change tracking permission in iOS Settings. When Google makes ad privacy options available, you can open them from the game settings. Granting tracking permission does not award game rewards; choosing to watch a rewarded ad is a separate action.

If you email us for support, we use your address and the details you provide to respond and resolve your request.
