How to playSupportTR EN

Two recipes,
one basket.

Send out your tiny collectors to gather ingredients from colorful cubes. Bring both recipes to the table before your basket fills up.

Explore the game

Your turn at the picnic

  1. Pick a color

    Send a collector from one of four queues. It gathers cubes of its own color that it can reach along the edge of the food.

  2. Make room in the basket

    Both recipes share a basket. Check which ingredients your recipe cards still need before choosing the next color.

  3. Serve a recipe

    Serve a card once you have all its ingredients. The ingredients leave the basket. Serve the second recipe to finish the level.

The picnic collection

Meet 31 food patterns across 250 levels, from watermelon to cake. Mystery boxes, linked boxes and timed lids change what you can collect next.

Picnic Time

Progress is saved on your device. An internet connection is required to play.
